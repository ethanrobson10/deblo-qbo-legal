Privacy Policy

Information practices for Deblo’s private internal QuickBooks Online integration.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Scope. This policy concerns Deblo Industries Ltd.’s private internal QuickBooks Online integration and the public legal-information pages on this website. The Application is intended for Deblo’s authorized business use, not for third-party customers.

1. Who we are

Deblo Industries Ltd. ("Deblo," "we," or "us") operates the Deblo QBO Integration (the "Application") to support its internal accounting operations. Questions about this policy can be sent to accounts@deblo.ca.

2. Information the Application may access

When an authorized Deblo administrator connects the Application to QuickBooks Online, it may access information reasonably needed for its enabled functions. This may include:

  • Company identifiers and connection information associated with Deblo’s QuickBooks Online account;
  • Customers’ and suppliers’ business contact information;
  • Invoices, credit memos, payments, balances, and related transaction details;
  • Other accounting records necessary for authorized reconciliation, reporting, or workflow functions; and
  • Technical information such as authentication tokens, connection status, and diagnostic records required to operate or troubleshoot the integration.

The precise information accessed depends on the Application features in use and the permissions authorized for the QuickBooks Online connection.

3. How information is used

Deblo uses information accessed through the Application only for legitimate internal activities, including accounts receivable monitoring, matching customer remittances to invoices and credits, reconciling payments, preparing accounting reports, troubleshooting integration issues, and maintaining appropriate accounting records. Application information is not intended to be sold or used for unrelated advertising.

4. Who may receive information

Information may be available to Deblo personnel authorized to perform relevant business functions. Deblo may also use service providers that support the Application’s hosting, data storage, backup, maintenance, and security, to the extent necessary to provide those services. Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect Deblo’s legitimate legal interests. The Application uses Intuit’s authorized connection to QuickBooks Online; Intuit’s own handling of QuickBooks data is governed by its separate policies.

5. Security and access

Deblo restricts Application access to authorized personnel and applies safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the business and accounting information involved. Safeguards and operational access arrangements may evolve as the Application is developed and maintained. Authorized users are expected to follow Deblo’s applicable confidentiality and information-security requirements. No method of data transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Location of processing

Depending on the infrastructure and providers used to operate the Application, data may be processed or stored in Canada or other jurisdictions. Information held outside Canada may be subject to the laws of the country where it is processed. Deblo reviews providers and relevant transfers before deploying new Application functionality.

7. Retention and deletion

Deblo retains Application information only as long as reasonably needed for the business purposes described here, operational records, troubleshooting, and applicable financial, tax, legal, or audit obligations. Working copies and technical records are removed when no longer needed under Deblo’s applicable retention practices. Disconnecting the Application from QuickBooks Online prevents further authorized access through that connection; it does not automatically remove records already retained for legitimate business or legal reasons.

8. Access, correction, and disconnection

Authorized personnel may contact accounts@deblo.ca with questions about Application access, personal information, correction, or deletion requests, subject to applicable legal and accounting recordkeeping requirements. A Deblo-authorized QuickBooks Online administrator may review and revoke the Application’s QuickBooks connection through the company’s app connection settings.

9. Public legal pages

These public legal-information pages are intended to be hosted through GitHub Pages. They contain no embedded advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies from Deblo. GitHub, as the website host, may process standard web request information under its own privacy practices.

10. Updates and contact

Deblo may update this policy as the Application, its service providers, or its information-handling practices change. The "Last updated" date above identifies the latest published revision. For privacy or Application questions, email accounts@deblo.ca.
