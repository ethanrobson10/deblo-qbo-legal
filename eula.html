End-user License Agreement

Terms for authorized users of Deblo’s internal QuickBooks Online accounting application.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Internal-use application. This agreement applies to the Deblo QBO Integration, a business tool intended for authorized users of Deblo Industries Ltd. It is not offered to the general public.

1. Parties and scope

This End-user License Agreement ("Agreement") governs use of the Deblo QBO Integration (the "Application"), an internal software application developed or operated by Deblo Industries Ltd. ("Deblo," "we," or "us") for Deblo’s own business accounting functions. This Agreement applies to Deblo employees and other individuals specifically authorized by Deblo to use the Application ("Users").

2. Permission to use

Subject to continuing authorization by Deblo, Users receive a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable permission to access and use the Application solely to perform authorized Deblo business activities. No permission is granted for personal use, use on behalf of unrelated businesses, or distribution of the Application to third parties.

3. User responsibilities

Users must comply with Deblo’s applicable internal policies and instructions, protect account credentials and access devices, and use the Application only within their assigned permissions. Users must promptly report suspected unauthorized access, erroneous transactions, or security concerns to Deblo. Users may not share their access, attempt to bypass controls, or export or disclose Deblo accounting information without appropriate authorization.

4. QuickBooks Online authorization

The Application may connect to Deblo’s QuickBooks Online company through Intuit’s authorized connection process. Only a Deblo-authorized administrator may approve or revoke that connection. QuickBooks Online is a separate service provided by Intuit and remains subject to Intuit’s applicable terms. This Application is not endorsed by, sponsored by, or affiliated with Intuit.

5. Accounting review and accuracy

The Application is intended to assist with internal accounting activities, including accounts receivable monitoring, invoice and payment matching, reconciliation, and related reporting. Outputs, suggested matches, or other automated results may be incomplete or inaccurate and must be reviewed through Deblo’s established accounting controls before being relied upon. Any capability that creates or changes a QuickBooks transaction must be used only with appropriate business authorization.

6. Confidentiality and information protection

Business, customer, supplier, and accounting information made available through the Application is confidential Deblo information. Users must handle it only for legitimate business purposes and in accordance with Deblo’s internal policies. The Privacy Policy describes the categories of information that may be accessed and the purposes for which they are used.

7. Availability and changes

The Application is an internal operational tool that may be modified, suspended, or discontinued by Deblo. Features may depend on the continued availability of QuickBooks Online and other supporting systems. Deblo may update this Agreement to reflect operational or legal changes.

8. Termination and disconnection

Deblo may suspend or end a User’s access at any time in accordance with its internal procedures. An authorized QuickBooks administrator may manage or disconnect the Application’s access within the QuickBooks Online app connection settings, or may contact accounts@deblo.ca for assistance. Disconnection stops future authorized access through that connection but does not necessarily delete accounting records retained for legitimate business or legal purposes.

9. Governing law and questions

This Agreement is governed by the applicable laws of Manitoba and Canada. Questions about authorized use or the Application may be sent to accounts@deblo.ca.
